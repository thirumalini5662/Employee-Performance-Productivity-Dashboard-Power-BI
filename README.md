# Employee Performance & Productivity Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Employee Performance & Productivity Dashboard is an interactive HR analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into employee performance, productivity, utilization rates, target achievement, training hours, and high-performing employees. It helps transform employee data into meaningful visual insights for workforce performance analysis.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee performance.
- Compare average performance ratings across departments.
- Analyze employee productivity categories.
- Monitor employee utilization rates.
- Examine target achievement in relation to utilization.
- Analyze training hours distribution.
- Track monthly utilization trends.
- Identify top-performing employees.
- Support data-driven workforce performance analysis.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Employees
- Average Performance Rating
- Average Utilization Rate
- Average Target Achievement
- Average Training Hours
- High Performers

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Average Performance Rating by Department
Compares the average employee performance rating across departments such as IT, Sales, Operations, Marketing, Finance, and HR.

### Productivity Category by Department
Shows the distribution of employees across High Productivity, Medium Productivity, and Low Productivity categories for each department.

### Training Hours Group
Analyzes employees based on training-hour groups:
- Low (<10)
- Medium (10–20)
- High (20+)

### Target Achievement vs Utilization
Uses a scatter plot to analyze the relationship between employee utilization rates and target achievement.

### Monthly Average Utilization Trend
Tracks the average employee utilization rate across different months to identify changes in workforce productivity over time.

### Top 10 Star Performers
Displays the top-performing employees along with their employee ID, name, department, performance rating, and utilization percentage.

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- Department
- Role
- Performance Category
- Training Hours Group

These filters allow users to explore employee performance from different perspectives.
